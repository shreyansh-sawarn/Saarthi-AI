## Agent Responsibilities

### Finance Health Agent
Analyzes customer spending, savings and financial wellness.

### Product Recommendation Agent
Identifies suitable SBI products including loans, deposits, investments and insurance.

### Digital Adoption Agent
Improves customer adoption of SBI digital services.

### Engagement Agent
Provides personalized insights, financial coaching and goal tracking.

### Banking Assistant Agent
Handles customer service requests and appointment management.

### Fraud Guardian Agent
Detects suspicious activities and promotes secure banking.
